This detached package builds on the four paused module copies in development300/pack_five317. Its directory name does not reserve a runtime version. It changes four existing modules and adds one production-path Lua fixture; no module loading, policy caps, runtime version, installation, settings, saves, native files or experimental authority is changed here.

One-choice Buffoon packs with an already owned active Yorick declare a third fixed policy once at the root, before offer outcomes are examined. The baseline and every offered endpoint share the same four composition worlds and all three policies. The existing two policies remain. The optional third policy considers one observed discard of exactly five eligible cards when the current play does not clear; it retains every required forced card and all Purple cards in the Certificate family. It takes its ordinary play path when five legal cards cannot be formed, cash is insufficient, no discard remains, or a current play clears. It never chooses a discard based on the later hidden draw. This is still one discard, up to four plays and one initial setup, not a search over all discard or play policies.

All work uses the original shared maximum 50,000 shop scores. Failure anywhere in an admitted policy leaves the family incomplete and cannot support the pack override.
