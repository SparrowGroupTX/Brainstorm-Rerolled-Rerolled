# Exact empty-pool Perkeo Gold cargo projection

This detached slice lets the existing final-boss Gold cargo comparison include
an owned or offered Perkeo only when every admitted endpoint proves an empty,
settled copying inventory. It may then replace an already completed Perkeo with
a genuinely missing Joker when the entire paid endpoint clears the existing
four common openings with at least 25% margin and no lower chips than both the
current row and the incumbent recommendation. It awards no sticker or win.

The original unconditional `stable_card(Perkeo)` remains false. Admission is
conditional on `modules.gold_perkeo`, exact vanilla identity/copy metadata, and
an independently recorded `shop_exit` certificate for every endpoint.

## Complete supported boundary

- Empty `consumeables` table, exactly zero pending buffer, known capacity.
- Visible, settled, distinct physical Joker identities; ordinary source
  Perkeo identity and Blueprint compatibility. Unknown perish timing rejects.
- Actual Blueprint/Brainstorm chains are recorded in the present order; zero
  generated cards is invariant under every legal later arrangement because
  there is no copying source card. Pinned order stays physically unchanged.
- Consumable capacity, cash, full inventory and playing-card population do not
  change at exit. Negative Joker purchase/sale capacity remains handled by the
  existing paid transition before the exit projection. Observatory has zero
  held Planets; there is no consumable available to use before the first hand.
- No free cash, invented first-hand use, assumed rearrangement, hidden random
  choice, or new score allowance is introduced. Existing Gold eligibility,
  Eternal/pinned sale protections, current incumbent and whole endpoint family
  remain required.

## Why homogeneous nonempty copying is deferred

The existing `strategy.inventory_value` enumerates a Polya-urn utility model
for small pools and explicitly labels its large-pool Observatory approximation.
It does not create a complete source-equivalent inventory or a legal first-hand
use/hold continuation. `phase_copy` may reorder before exit to increase copies;
the ordinary shop scorer copies the inventory supplied to it and does not
execute Perkeo's shop-exit callback. Simply permitting Perkeo would therefore
compare different actual post-exit inventories as though they were unchanged.

Identical card names alone do not repair that gap: ordinary and Negative input
cards may have different abilities/metadata, each new copy becomes part of the
subsequent pool, and source copying/edition application reprices cards and adds
capacity. Matching Planets also change Observatory multipliers, while the
pre-blind advisor can choose to use a copy. Those choices must remain explicit
and share the same paid cash, population and scoring worlds.

A later nonempty extension needs (1) exact copy-equivalence over all copied
fields, (2) original source copy/edition/capacity/pricing evidence, (3) the actual
executable phase arrangement and event order, and (4) a complete bounded
post-exit hold/use family against the current recommendation. Each random
non-equivalent pool branch must either be evaluated jointly or rejected. No
such original-source probe or policy replay was authorized/executed here.
