# 287 — complete blind progress can satisfy a shop-sequence scoring gate

`shop_sequences.lua` previously used opening score for its emergency bonus, a hard ratio veto below 0.85, and a final 10% opening-gain requirement even when `shop_scoring.lua` had already completed paired whole-blind policies. A build whose first hand is weaker but whose complete policy progresses farther could therefore be excluded by those opening-only checks. This is a current-code comparison gap. ## Narrow exception and retained safeguards

The existing graph still contains at most two visible Joker/Planet buys, one original Joker sale and one Planet use. State limits, shared scoring budgets, complete graph expansion, the incumbent first action's inclusion, exact transitions, capacity, purchase costs, repricing, protected inventory, liquidity and the previous strategic merit thresholds remain unchanged. At least two actions, merit 26, and an eight-point advantage over the best incumbent-first continuation remain required. The result still exposes only the next user-clicked action and asks for fresh advice afterwards.

The new exception requires complete supported known-mechanics finishing evidence for the root and **every evaluated endpoint in the admitted graph**, including endpoints that do not become the winning plan. Every certificate has four complete worlds, a consistent positive target, validated action counts and finite progress/cash/population/reward fields. Each endpoint's before-worlds must exactly match the root certificate; a changed target, missing world, unknown outcome, malformed action or different before-profile prevents the exception.

The candidate must improve progress strictly in at least one world against both saving and the best complete continuation whose first action matches the incumbent recommendation. In every paired world it must preserve existing clears, not lower capped progress, use no more hands or discards, and require no more total actions after counting both the visible shop sequence and actual play/discard actions. Population loss and finishing reward cannot worsen. A reference world that clears additionally requires known Blue quantity/hand/utility and survivor evidence; a candidate cannot replace a Blue reward or physical survivor with a larger aggregate reward number. Equal progress alone does not qualify this slice.

Cash compares the actual post-play balance after allowing only the exact difference between the two already-paid shop endpoint balances. For example, a $3 buy/use endpoint may start the blind $3 below saving, but may not hide an additional scored-dollar loss. Payment remains explicit in `additional_paid_cash`, `endpoint_cash_before` and `endpoint_cash_after`; a negative difference means the candidate paid less. This exception does not fabricate interest, cashout or future offers. Existing purchase/interest/liquidity penalties still price the actual spending, and protected Perkeo/Observatory/Negative inventory rules still govern transitions. The certificate is a bounded progress/resource comparison, not proof of superior lifetime development or an optimal spending policy.

When the candidate satisfies these conditions, its complete mean-progress gain can supply the existing deficit bonus, the complete evidence can satisfy the sub-0.85 opening-ratio gate, and the final complete comparison can satisfy the 10% opening-gain gate. No weights, score limits, worlds or policies are added. Incomplete families retain the incumbent instead of promoting a favorable partial certificate.
