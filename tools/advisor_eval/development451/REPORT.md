## Change and limits

The new mode compares the actual incumbent first play and a complete greedy
current-play hold alternative with applying each of one or two eligible ordinary
Planets and then the SAME incumbent first play. It handles up to eight held cards,
eight Jokers/eight consumables and120 remaining deck cards. Black Hole expansion
is excluded. Existing tactical actions, current clears and discard-mode behavior
retain priority. This changes neither general Perkeo retention nor discard policy.

Each branch uses the same four deterministic public-composition worlds, exact
physical owned use/Negative capacity, full retained inventory/Observatory, actual
first-play transitions and automatic refills. Final plays use only their observed
hand; every legal subset in the existing current-order family is scored. This is
not a search over all card/Joker permutations, all possible first plays over the
full horizon, later owned uses or the entire run. Unknown/uncertain scores,
unsupported considered transitions and exhausted budgets invalidate the whole
comparison. Physical IDs, visibility and resource consistency are checked.

The existing inventory-cost conversion remains explicit: required paired uplift
is at least0.125, plus action/discard costs, plus inventory points/100. The cost
comes from the complete remaining Perkeo pool and Observatory valuation. The
selected use must beat the best compared hold plan and the incumbent. Four worlds
and that conversion are heuristics, not calibrated survival probabilities. A
selected action uses one card, then refreshes from the actual observation.
12,000 specialist and140,000 aggregate caps,25,000 consumable cap and all other
limits remain unchanged.
