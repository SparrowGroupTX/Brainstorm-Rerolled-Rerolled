## Proposed top ten, provisional order

1. **Support known blind-start transitions in tactical build comparisons.**
   Dagger and Marble currently appear in shop_scoring.skip_rows, causing the
   comparison to fall back for permanently owned starters in Knife, Medusa and
   Monolith. Model supported ordered destruction/addition and resources with
   original-source parity, identical legal setup freedom and explicit unknowns.
   Acceptance: meaningful before/after choices for those structures receive
   correct evidence without inventing cards, income or retained engines.
2. **Calibrate earlier build decisions using the new verified replay tools.**
   Find the earliest avoidable score deficit in representative frozen requests;
   compare genuinely offered/affordable alternatives and bounded survival/growth/
   spending coefficients. Validate the evaluation profile independently of user
   saves, source mechanics and unseen terminal cohorts. Existing replay plumbing
   is complete; this item is using it to identify and validate a better policy.
3. **Plan short sequences of visible shop actions.** Compare legal buy+buy,
   sell+buy+use, Planet+Joker and save outcomes together instead of ranking one
   purchase at a time. Preserve debt, inflation, slot locks, interest and Perkeo
   inventory. Acceptance: complementary affordable upgrades can beat isolated
   filler, while incomplete comparisons fall back as a whole.
4. **Evaluate real bounded finishing sequences.** Share an evaluator between
   ambiguous shop readiness and hand decisions that carries actual plays,
   discards, deck depletion, growth and owned consumables. Expand selected rescue
   branches beyond the final playable hand. Keep the70-score fast clear and only
   spend the added budget where complete paired outcomes can change the choice.
5. **Score deck-changing and non-Joker pack decisions tactically.** Existing
   exact Tarot/Spectral/card transformations need paired composition comparisons
   and usable immediate/next-blind outcomes; current shop scoring rejects changed
   populations and non-Joker packs mostly use strategic ratings. Preserve Glass,
   population, Kings and Perkeo/Observatory valuation. Especially relevant to
   Jokerless, Fragile, Medusa and Bram Poker.
6. **Value rerolls by useful improvement size and failed-search cost.** Replace
   counting merely relevant Jokers with source-supported chances of an affordable
   shortfall-reducing upgrade. Budget purchases, rent, paid discards and failed
   refreshes; never promise a specific unseen offer. Validate actual action changes
   with held-out decisions, not only pool-probability calculations.
7. **Model additional conditional growth/generation opportunities.** Source-check
   actual trigger paths, legal generated consumable pools and payout timing for
   currently unsupported effects. Balance development against the retained finish;
   handle sampled randomness without granting optimistic growth. This extends,
   rather than repeats, current conditional_value and exact Yorick/Burnt work.
8. **Add decisions for concealed-card uncertainty.** Use known composition and
   observed information for future face-down cards instead of rejecting all such
   continuations or optimizing hidden identities. Preserve common deterministic
   samples. Main benefit: X-ray and concealed-card boss situations across runs.
9. **Remove demonstrated repeated scoring work.** Use component profiles to prove
   full state keys/invalidation before extra reuse, and compare complete matched
   actions/results with latency distributions. Current cache results are mixed;
   a runtime expansion needs evidence. Most benefit is lower time per attempt,
   not necessarily higher win probability.
10. **Choose filtered openings by retained builds and full completion cost.**
    Compare matched later blind boundaries, useful surviving copy targets,
    Dagger scoring transfer, misses and total setup/actions/compute time. Follow
    with bounded terminal attempts before promoting a filter. Jokerless excluded.
    Acquisition of two Legendary names is insufficient.

These rankings are judgments about expected benefit versus engineering and
runtime cost, not measured causal ROI or additive percentage-point promises.
Items1/4/5/7 share transition work;2/3/6 share build evaluation/calibration.
Instrumentation and filter search can lower expected time without raising p.
The objective remains sum over challenges of expected attempt cost divided by
success probability under stationary independent retry assumptions, including
setup/search costs with the appropriate attempt definition. Hardest challenges
can dominate total retries despite an improving average.
