# Certificate in complete bounded shop/pack policies

Detached proposal; parent owns runtime merge, release and installed validation.
No captured policy replay, new source execution, or player-data access occurred.

## Behavior

The current advisor falls back the entire opened-pack comparison when
Certificate is visible because its generation transition is missing. C03's
preserved postmortem shows why that matters: Card Sharp had complete repeat-hand
trajectory evidence, but Certificate's unsupported branch forced both choices
back to generic ratings. This slice makes a narrow whole family comparable.

For each of the existing four common ordinary-deck worlds, draw the normal
initial hand first, then append one ordinary Certificate card directly to hand
and public population. Capacity stays unchanged, and the original remaining
deck is identical. Four fixed front ordinals and Purple/Gold/Blue/Red seal
classes define four generation composition samples. They do not cover all208
front/seal outcomes, predict source RNG, or measure winning probability.

All admitted endpoints complete the existing play-only and one targeted
observed-discard policies in those four worlds. The targeted policy retains
**all Purple cards on discard**, including Purple cards originally in the deck;
they may still be played normally. This restriction applies to the entire
declared root family, including alternatives without Certificate. The existing
discard generator enumerates its complete observed legal subset family under
that constraint, then chooses its best target before seeing replacement cards.
It does not filter based on later survival. If a forced Purple card prevents
discarding, the policy proceeds without that discard.

The ordinary `scoring.after_discard` Purple guard remains unchanged. A Hook or
another effect that actually discards Purple without a supplied Tarot outcome
still makes the comparison unsupported. No Tarot is invented or credited to
Perkeo. Blue/Gold end-of-round valuation retains its existing disclosed scope;
future Planets/income are never spent during the blind.

The nine-card initial hand is admitted only with the bound generation receipt:
same capacity, one generated physical id present in the conserved population,
and matching count. Ordinary replacement draws continue filling to actual
capacity. Normal worlds retain the eight-card boundary; no search cap increases.
Every new subset and transition is charged to the existing50,000 shared budget.

## Boundaries

Exactly one active physical Certificate; active Blueprint/Brainstorm or a
second Certificate reject rather than collapse correlated outcomes. Combined
setting-blind generators/Chicot and Bell forced-card generation remain outside
the scope. Unknown loaded front/base metadata, pending/nonempty Perkeo copying,
hidden observations, malformed growth, or unsupported future actions reject.

`certificate.capture` reads loaded public definitions only when Certificate is
owned or visible in shop/pack. It requires exactly52 unique canonical ordinary
rank/suit fronts and an unmodified c_base center. No saves, profile files or
future seeds are read. The source population and prototype must not be filled
in from an assumed complete account or fabricated captured-state metadata.

Hologram's exact physical playing-card-added growth is applied before adding
the generated card. Other derived deck counters refresh from the actual new
population. First-hand generation uncertainty remains explicit in comparison
receipts even when the finite admitted continuation family completes.

## Receipts for cross-choice policy comparison

Each `compare()` result now includes `common_worlds` with schema1, kind
`shop_four_common_worlds_v1`, samples4, ordinal `world_ids={1,2,3,4}`, the exact
ordinary draw seeds/model, continuation definition, shared discard policy and
generation model. Its `family_key` is an exact length-delimited deterministic
encoding of the root public data, contract, draw permutations and order limit.
It excludes only computed `_shop_scoring`/`_readiness` caches. It is neither a
lossy hash nor a changing process counter. A consumer comparing separate choices
must require equal keys and matching contracts; missing non-data/cyclic keys
are ineligible. Completed finishing trajectories carry `composition_world_id`.

`generation_only_uncertainty` is true only when every uncertain endpoint has
the supported Certificate generation samples and no scoring uncertainty or
conditional temporal assumptions. Certificate visibility alone cannot admit
unrelated random scoring or an incomplete future trajectory.

Each clearing trajectory preserves `finish_details` separately from the scalar
`finish_reward`: held Gold dollars, Blue count and exact Planet key/hand, the
ordinary slots before and after Blue generation, complete pre/post-play
consumable inventory, original capacity/buffer, and cash after the played hand.
`held_supported`, `blue_supported`, `final_blind` and the existing heuristic
utility marker remain visible. `cashout_joker_income_modeled=false` for any
Joker row: the module does not invent complete Joker payouts from successful
scoring. Consumers must separately qualify such income or reject the proposed
resource-priority substitution. Losing worlds have no manufactured cashout
receipt. No Blue Planet is used or sold before the sampled blind clears.
