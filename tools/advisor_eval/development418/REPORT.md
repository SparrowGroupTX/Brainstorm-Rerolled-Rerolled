## What changed

1. **Additive effect families.** Canonical Flower Pot, Even Steven and Greedy Joker
   can now participate in retained-clear proofs. Ice Cream, Fortune Teller, Devious
   Joker and Card Sharp qualify their actual main-stage fields. Canonical Negative
   Joker editions add no scoring arithmetic; Gold playing cards and Red retriggers
   are admitted where their actual effects preserve the order proof. Altered values,
   unknown fields, mixed editions, Wild and card-stage multipliers remain outside.

2. **Hanging Chad's worst legal order.** Discarding can change which retained card
   scores first. The new proof checks every possible first scoring card by rotating
   each selected physical card to the front. Under the qualified additive contract,
   the other scoring-card additions commute. The same actual scorer handles copies,
   Red retriggers, debuffed first scorers and non-scoring kickers. The complete
   family's minimum must clear; even one losing member rejects the discard.
   Every score uses the existing12-call allowance. Incomplete work is not a floor.

3. **Hidden spare cards.** A known visible hand can now survive discarding concealed
   spares when the complete public population and Joker row establish identity-
   independent discard behavior. The hidden identities are never selected as play
   anchors, recovered or assigned to their physical slots. Active Burnt, identity-
   sensitive callbacks, unknown effects and possible Purple/Blue/Gold/held-Gold
   rewards prevent that permission. Paid and forced-hidden discards remain blocked.
   Fresh observations retain unknown discarded identities in the public population.

4. **Acorn integration guard.** Acorn's existing later-world check uses one fixed
   scoring order. It now explicitly refuses a Chad first-scorer certificate rather
   than silently applying it to unproved Joker orders. That full product family
   remains future work; the game is not told it has a guarantee it lacks.

Only growth.lua, acorn_discard.lua and two version stamps change relative to the
installed416 release. Search, scorer, executor, Bell behavior and work caps stay
unchanged. No captured observation was evaluated with policy/scorer or made into
a fixture; all execution tests use invented cards and mocked physical settlements.
