## Concrete gap and repair

Gold goal comparison previously admitted only Violet Vessel and Verdant Leaf; simply adding Cerulean Bell to that list would omit its forced-card legality. The new helper collects every legal held subset already scored by the ordinary shop opening comparison. For each physical held identity it retains the best legal subset that includes that identity. This yields a complete family of possible first forced-card outcomes without another score call. A direct synthetic oracle sets the real scorer's `ability.forced_selection` flag separately for each card and agrees with the reused subset results.

The shop selects one legal fixed Joker layout across all four common composition worlds, using the mean of the per-world forced-card floors. It never selects a different layout for each sampled draw or forced card. Each receipt records the physical layout, actual zero/one setup action, four complete forced families, exact physical population and held identities, played-card limit, subset coverage and target. Scalar opening APIs remain available but explicitly identify a lower bound over the first forced choice. Later forced selections and full-blind continuations remain unresolved. Known blind-start creation, destruction and disabling remain outside this Bell extension.

Gold's complete paid endpoint family keeps its cash, capacity, inventory, protected-population, original-sale, unique-missing-key and exact-incumbent guards. Bell admission additionally verifies all four complete receipts, a 25% margin for every forced-card branch, and nonnegative score delta for the same forced identity against both hold and the exact incumbent. ## Separate prerequisite found by a realistic fixture

`shop_sequences.copy` used `type(v)~='function' and v or nil`, which silently converted explicitfalse values into absent fields. Ordinary snapshots emit `face_down=false`; after a harmless Joker buy the protected playing-card population would no longer equal its input, so Gold declined the entire family. The small prerequisite preserves all nonfunction scalar values, includingfalse and0. Function exclusion and deep-copy isolation remain intact. The fixture preserves explicitfalse in playing cards, consumables and nested metadata, verifies exact original population equality and checks that changing the detached result does not mutate the input.
